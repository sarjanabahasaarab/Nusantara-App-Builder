# Nusantara App Builder — Production Templates

This directory contains standard project templates provided out of the box with Nusantara App Builder (Phase 10).

## Available Templates:
1. **blank-app**: Minimal Android Material 3 starter for custom visual design.
2. **website-app**: Web-to-App WebView native wrapper with hardware acceleration and AdMob integration.
3. **business-app**: Corporate & SME portal with product catalog and contact actions.
4. **school-app**: School & Education management with offline timetables and announcement feeds.
5. **news-app**: News portal reader with category filtering, article bookmarks, and AdMob Native ads.
6. **food-app**: Culinary restaurant digital menu with cart and table ordering.
7. **store-app**: Mobile storefront with SQLite local catalog, shopping cart, and QRIS payment UI.
8. **portfolio-app**: Creator & developer personal showcase with skills grid and resume viewer.
9. **api-app**: Pre-configured Retrofit 2 & OkHttp REST API client with swipe-to-refresh.
10. **sqlite-app**: Offline-first Room / SQLite database with CRUD tables and full-text search.
